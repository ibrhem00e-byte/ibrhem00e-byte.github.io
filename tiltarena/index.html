Tilt Arena

Push them off. Be the last one standing.

Tilt Arena is a fast 4-player battle on a tilting disc: push your rivals off the edge, parry their pushes, and be the last one standing. Play ranked online, with friends in a party, or against the computer.

Support

Questions, a bug, a purchase that didn't arrive, or a player to report? Email us at tiltarena.support@gmail.com and include your player ID number (shown on your profile in the game). We usually answer within 2 days.

Frequently asked

  • How do I keep my progress on a new phone? Open your profile and link your account with Google. On the new phone, link the same account and choose to bring back your saved progress.
  • How do I play with friends? Give a friend your ID number (shown in the Friends box), or type theirs to add them. Then tap Invite next to their name. Everyone presses Ready, and the party leader presses Play.
  • How do I delete my account? In the game: Settings > Delete Account. See delete your account.
