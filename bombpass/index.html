Bomb Pass

Catch it, throw it, don't let it blow!

Bomb Pass is a fast 4-player bomb-passing battle: catch the bomb, pick your target and throw it before it explodes in your hands. Play ranked online, with friends in a party, or against bots.

Support

Questions, a bug, a purchase that didn't arrive, or a player to report? Email us at bombpass1@gmail.com and include your player ID number (shown on your profile in the game). We usually answer within 2 days.

Frequently asked

  • How do I keep my progress on a new phone? Open your profile and link your account with Google (Android) or Apple (iPhone). On the new phone, link the same account and choose to load your saved progress.
  • I bought gems but didn't get them. Close and reopen the game while connected to the internet: unfinished purchases are delivered on start. If they still don't arrive, email us with your player ID.
  • How do I delete my account? In the game: Settings > Delete account. See delete your account.
